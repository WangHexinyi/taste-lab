# V4.4 Composition & Motion Experiment

This version keeps the successful V4.3 spatial-media policy and changes the next bottleneck revealed by the KADO / 01 benchmark.

## Hypothesis

The current system can now choose and execute true 3D when spatial value is real, but models still over-literalize style adjectives and fall into familiar AI visual archetypes. They also tend to repeat max-width/grid/card section skeletons and invest in ambient/object effects while leaving scroll narrative continuity weak.

V4.4 tests whether stronger **category promise**, **composition rhythm**, **motion grammar**, and **anti-AI archetype** reasoning can improve perceived authorship without turning the Skill into a fixed layout or animation template.

## Controlled changes

1. Category Promise before adjective literalism.
2. Anti-AI archetype firewall for recurring visual clusters.
3. Composition Rhythm Engine based on scale/density/alignment/media/container changes rather than fixed layouts.
4. Motion Grammar separating ambient/object/state/narrative/transition roles.
5. A default narrative-motion baseline for expressive multi-section surfaces, without mandating one fade-up recipe.
6. Stable V4.3 3D-neutral, opportunity-aware spatial policy retained.
7. QA fixes for first-viewport media/CTA geometry and automated QA-state synchronization.

## What this version intentionally does NOT do

- It does not ban dark interfaces, mono typography, grids, beige editorial layouts, cards, 3D, or particles.
- It does not require asymmetry, full-bleed media, or scroll animation on utility surfaces.
- It does not force GSAP, Three.js, WebGL, or any specific framework.
- It does not prescribe exact palettes, fonts, section orders, or animation values.

Judge the experiment by the rendered result and cross-model repeatability.
