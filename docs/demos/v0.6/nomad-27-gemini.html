Curatorial Framework

A coastal topography of fragmented frequencies, somatic resonance, and machine memory.

Dr. Maya Lin-Sorensen Chief Curator, Moving Image & Digital
Elena Vance Spatial Sound & Choreographic Lead

In our hyper-mediated coastal ecology, information arrives as high-frequency pulses—light refracted through salt fog, sonar scanning seabed fissures, and data streams circulating through underwater fibre optics. NOMAD / 27 positions itself squarely in the interstitial spaces: the quiet interval between a soundwave and its architectural reflection, between a camera lens and a living nervous system.

Over seven days, the festival repurposes four radical coastal architectures—a defunct heavy turbine hall, a cliff-edge glass pavilion, a subterranean bomb-shelter amphitheatre, and an open tide square—transforming them into acoustic transducers and luminous projection chambers.

Spatial Geography

FOUR SITES

The festival activates 4 architectural extremes along a 3.4km coastal corridor connected by electric sea shuttles.

SITE 01 / INDUSTRIAL

THE TURBINE HALL

Former 1932 Coal Power Station

Raw 24-meter concrete ceilings and industrial reverberation, hosting monumental spatial installations and modular synthesizer performances.

CAPACITY: 1,400 ACOUSTICS: 4.8s Decay Time ACCESS: North Pier Ferry
SITE 02 / GLASS ARCHITECTURE

COASTAL PAVILION

Cliff-Edge Glass Art Museum

Cantilevered directly over the ocean breakers, housing expanded cinema projections that dissolve into the maritime twilight horizon.

CAPACITY: 450 PROJECTION: 4K Laser + 35mm ACCESS: Cliffside Boardwalk
SITE 03 / SUBTERRANEAN

THE VAULTS

Underground Naval Munitions Bunker

Deep subterranean darkness with zero natural light. Dedicated to pitch-black spatial sound immersion and multichannel wavefield synthesis.

CAPACITY: 300 SOUND: 64.4 Ambisonic Array ACCESS: South Tunnel Gate
SITE 04 / PUBLIC HORIZON

TIDE SQUARE

Intertidal Stone Esplanade

Submerged at spring tide, this open civic arena features outdoor choreographic rituals, laser atmospheric interventions, and free public concerts.

CAPACITY: 3,500 (Open) ELEMENT: Wind & Tide Dependent ACCESS: Central Promenade
