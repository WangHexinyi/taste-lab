# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Taste Lab a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, reproducible critique and separate evidence from taste preference.
- Be respectful of different backgrounds, tools and levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, Issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Instances may be reported privately through the repository's security advisory channel. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.
